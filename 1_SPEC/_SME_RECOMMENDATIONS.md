## Screen 2 - MATRA_BUILD  जल -> जा -> जाल

*Deck slide 5 · mockup `_SME_MOCKUPS/slide05_image5.png`*

> Recommendation
> VO:
> “आइए, देखें कि आ की मात्रा लगने से शब्द की आवाज़ कैसे बदलती है।”
> पहले:
> “यह शब्द है — जल।”
> फिर:
> “ज में आ की मात्रा लगाने पर — जा बनता है।”
> अंत में:
> “अब जल में आ की मात्रा लगाने पर — जाल बनता है।”
> On Screen:
> Keep the screen focused on only one transformation. 
> First show जल clearly on the left. 
> Then show the transformation in the centre:ज + ा = जा 
> After that, complete the word as:जाल 
> Highlight only the ा matra when it is introduced. 
> A simple relevant image can support the final word जाल. 
> Sequence:
> जल appears. 
> VO reads: “जल”. 
> The letter ज is highlighted. 
> ा appears beside ज. 
> Show:ज + ा = जा 
> Play the sound difference clearly:“ज” → “जा” 
> Then ल joins. 
> Final word appears:जाल 
> Highlight ा inside जाल. 
> Final image appears. 
> Animation:
> Use simple step-by-step animation. 
> जल should appear first. 
> Then isolate or highlight ज. 
> The ा matra should slide/pop into place beside ज. 
> As soon as it joins, ज visually transforms into जा. 
> Then ल joins to form जाल. 
> Keep the sound transformation synced with the visual:ज → जा → जाल 
> Avoid showing all stages together at the start. 
> Sound Differentiation:
> Make the difference between the sounds very clear: 
> ज 
> जा 
> जाल 
> Give a small pause between each sound so the child can hear the change. 
> The main learning focus should be:आ की मात्रा जुड़ने पर ज की आवाज़ “जा” हो जाती है। 
> SFX:
> Soft pop when ा appears. 
> Light chime when ज changes to जा. 
> Small success sound when जाल is completed. 
> Keep SFX subtle so the pronunciation remains clear. 
> Next Button:
> Keep Next disabled while the transformation is playing. 
> Activate it only after ज → जा → जाल is fully demonstrated. 
> Note:
> Keep the screen clean and minimal. 
> Do not add extra explanatory text. 
> Focus on the before-matra and after-matra sound difference. 
> The key visual learning should be: 
> जल → ज + ा = जा → जाल

## Screen 13 - MATRA_FILL  fill the blank

*Deck slide 16 · mockup `_SME_MOCKUPS/slide16_image16.png`*

> Recommendation
> This activity will be a reverse matra-building train activity.
> In this screen, three train coaches will be shown.Each coach will have an incomplete word with a blank space where the missing matra should be added.
> The child will drag the correct matra from the options given below and drop it into the blank space inside the correct coach.As soon as the child drops the correct matra, the word will be completed.
> Use these words:

## Screen 15 - POEM_SEARCH  मात्रा खोजो

*Deck slide 18 · mockup `_SME_MOCKUPS/slide18_image18.png`*

> Recommendation
> Activity Concept
> Use this as a final “मात्रा खोजो” challenge.
> Keep the focus only on:
> poem card 
> magnifying glass 
> target words 
> ghost animation feedback 
> Remove extra decorative elements like:
> Ravi 
> kite 
> girl 
> tree 
> unnecessary background objects 
> Use a clean version of the existing UI so the child’s attention stays on the activity.
> Poem on Screen
> रवि लाया लाल पतंग,दिन में चमकी सूरज की किरण।नीम तले मीना गाए संग।
> VO Flow
> Round 1 VO:“आ की मात्रा वाले शब्द ढूँढो।”
> Round 2 VO:“अब छोटी इ की मात्रा वाले शब्द ढूँढो।”
> Round 3 VO:“अब बड़ी ई की मात्रा वाले शब्द ढूँढो।”
> Target Words
> For आ की मात्रा (ा):
> लाया 
> लाल 
> For छोटी इ की मात्रा (ि):
> रवि 
> दिन 
> किरण 
> For बड़ी ई की मात्रा (ी):
> नीम 
> मीना 
> Magnifying Glass Interaction
> A large magnifying glass appears at the bottom-right. 
> Child drags the magnifying glass over the poem. 
> Any word under the glass slightly enlarges. 
> Child taps/selects the word through the magnifying glass. 
> Correct words remain highlighted. 
> Ghost Animation
> Use a small friendly ghost character/effect as a search guide, not as decoration.
> Entry animation:
> When the activity begins, a small ghost floats in from one side. 
> It briefly circles around the magnifying glass. 
> Then it fades slightly or moves to a corner. 
> During search:
> If the child is idle for a few seconds, the ghost appears near the magnifying glass and gently moves toward the poem. 
> This works as a visual cue: “start searching”. 
> On correct answer:
> Ghost pops up happily near the correct word. 
> Small sparkle appears. 
> Ghost can do a short happy bounce and disappear. 
> VO: “शाबाश!” 
> On 1st wrong attempt:
> Wrong word gives a small wiggle/shake. 
> Ghost briefly appears with a thinking expression. 
> No hand nudge. 
> VO: “फिर से देखो।” 
> On 2nd wrong attempt:
> Wrong word shakes again. 
> Ghost floats toward one correct target word. 
> It gently points/pulses near that word. 
> This acts as the hint instead of adding extra text. 
> VO: “ध्यान से मात्रा पहचानो।” 
> Round Completion
> When all target words for one matra are found:
> Correct words glow together. 
> Ghost flies across the selected words with a sparkle trail. 
> Soft success chime plays. 
> Next target VO starts automatically. 
> Final Completion
> After all three matra rounds:
> All correct words stay highlighted. 
> Ghost appears once in the centre, celebrates with a small spin/sparkle. 
> Magnifying glass gives a final glow. 
> Next button becomes active. 
> Animation
> Poem card fades in first. 
> Magnifying glass slides in from the bottom. 
> Ghost enters after the magnifying glass. 
> Keep ghost motion slow and soft, not distracting. 
> Do not keep ghost constantly moving on screen. 
> UI/UX Note
> Keep the screen minimal:
> plain/light background 
> central poem card 
> magnifying glass 
> small ghost 
> mascot/audio button if required 
> next button 
> Remove the large scenic illustrations. The main learning focus should be:
> VO target → scan poem → find correct word → tap → highlight
